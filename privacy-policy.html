MsgLedger Privacy Policy
Effective date: 19 November 2026
Developer: Abhiram K Suryaprakash
Contact: abhiramksuryaprakash@gmail.com

Messaging and local data
MsgLedger is a default SMS application. After you choose it as the default SMS handler and grant SMS permissions, it reads and stores SMS on this phone, receives new SMS and MMS, sends your text replies, and lets you mark, archive, block or delete messages. System SMS access stops when MsgLedger is no longer the default handler. Previously imported records remain locally until you delete them or clear app data.
Message content, OTPs, personal phone numbers, contacts, financial amounts and budgets are not uploaded to MsgLedger's usage service or logo services. Spending extraction and categorisation run on the phone. They may be inaccurate; you can correct or exclude expenses. MsgLedger does not access your bank account.

Contacts, SIMs and biometrics
Optional Contacts permission displays names, pictures, contact suggestions and Android favourites. Optional write access changes a contact's favourite status only when you ask. Optional Phone permission identifies SIM subscriptions for filtering and sending; the app does not read your call log. Android handles biometric authentication for optional message and finance locks. The message lock protects in-app inbox, conversation, composer and MMS viewing; it does not hide notification previews. MsgLedger does not obtain or store fingerprint templates. A screen lock is not encryption of the local database.

Basic usage counter
Basic usage reporting is enabled by default. The first privacy notice, About and Settings let you turn it off. With reporting on, the app sends a randomly generated installation identifier and app version over HTTPS to https://msgledger-counter.abhiramksuryaprakash.workers.dev, at most once per day when the app is opened. The developer operates this service using Cloudflare Workers and a D1 database. The application database stores a SHA-256 hash of the identifier, first-seen and last-seen timestamps, and app version. The public counter shows only a total of reporting installations active in the last 30 days, not a count of individual people.
Turning reporting off stops heartbeats and requests deletion of that installation's counter record. If offline, deletion remains queued and retries when online. A cached aggregate may take time to refresh. The current counter has no automatic expiry of database records; records remain until deleted. Uninstalling alone does not send a deletion request. Turn reporting off while online before uninstalling if you want its record removed. Reinstallation may create a new identifier.
Cloudflare receives standard connection metadata, including your IP address, when serving requests. Platform security, infrastructure logs and backup retention are governed by Cloudflare's service practices and the developer's account configuration; the app does not intentionally add IP addresses or message content to the counter database.

Online sender logos
Bundled bank logos work offline. Optional online logo lookup sends known company names to Wikidata and Google Play search, and company website domains to Google's favicon service and selected official company websites. For missing images, MsgLedger may inspect a limited number of public Google Play app listings, compare their company/publisher and website information, and download a matching icon from Google's image servers. The app uses a neutral app identifier in these requests and does not enumerate installed apps or use your Play account. Uncertain matches remain unresolved. Lookup is limited and may stop temporarily if public pages change or throttle requests. These requests retrieve company artwork; personal phone numbers, full message text and OTPs are not included. Internet services receive normal request metadata, such as the requesting IP address, and apply their own privacy practices. Turn online downloads off under Settings > Sender logos to stop new requests. Downloaded images are cached on the phone. A custom logo selected through Gallery or Android's file picker can be positioned and resized in a local preview, with optional outer-white-background removal. It is saved locally as a transparent PNG and is not published or shared with other users. Recognition is not verification of a sender's identity.

Carrier messaging, links and user actions
Your carrier and Android's telephony services process SMS and incoming MMS delivery. Carrier charges and mobile data usage may apply. Tapping a message link opens the website in your chosen browser; its operator then handles that visit. Opening an attachment in another app grants that app temporary access to the selected file. Forwarding opens an editable SMS draft; sending it shares its content with the recipient through your carrier. Support email contains the information you choose to send and is processed by your email provider and the developer's Gmail service.

Local message export
You can export all message records currently saved in MsgLedger, including original sender headers, message text, timestamps, folders and SIM/read/archive metadata, to a JSON file through Android's save-file picker. The file is not encrypted. Saved MMS text is included; media attachments, contacts, saved expenses, budgets and app settings are excluded. This version provides export, not restoration. Choose a folder under On this device for a local file. If you choose a cloud storage provider in the Android picker, that provider may upload and process the file under its own practices. MsgLedger does not automatically upload the exported messages to its servers. The file remains in your chosen location after uninstalling until you delete it yourself. A temporary private cache file is used during export and removed when the operation finishes.

Storage, deletion and security
Local messages, expenses, budgets, sender preferences and caches are stored in the app's private storage. Android app backup is disabled. The app does not implement cross-device sync. Optional automatic deletion is off until you choose a period. All-message deletion affects the Android SMS/MMS inbox on both SIMs, not just MsgLedger's view. Deleting a message can retain its saved expense; deleting or excluding an expense adjusts the local spending total. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove remaining private app data; this does not itself erase your carrier/system inbox or notify the usage service. Android and carrier data require their own deletion controls.
HTTPS protects the app's internet requests in transit. No system is guaranteed completely secure. There are no advertisements, advertising SDKs, or sale of personal data in this release. Contact the developer for privacy questions or assistance with a deletion request. There is no user account to delete. The app does not intentionally target children.
