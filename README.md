MsgLedger is an Android SMS app that brings everyday messaging, organised conversations and spending insights together. Read and send messages, find important OTPs, track purchases from banking SMS and manage monthly budgets—all in one place.

Organised messaging

Keep your inbox easy to navigate with dedicated Transactions, Personal and Promotions tabs. Additional folders, including banking, services, government messages and credit cards, are available through a dropdown.

MsgLedger recognises sender headers and message content to help sort incoming SMS. You can also mark a sender as promotional so future messages go to Promotions, with promotional notifications kept silent.

Everyday conversations

View saved contact names and photos, search conversations and compose messages using contact-name suggestions. Android favourite contacts are available for quick messaging, with support for choosing between multiple numbers saved under one contact.

The conversation screen includes dated message bubbles, quick replies, selectable text and tappable web links. A floating compose button makes starting a new message easy.

Clear OTP notifications

MsgLedger recognises OTPs and phrases such as verification code, sign-in code and temporary code. Notifications make detected codes easier to read and copy, show receipt times and distinguish the latest OTP from earlier ones.

Message notifications include mark-as-read and delete actions. Their appearance and available controls depend on Android and your device’s notification settings.

Spending insights from SMS

MsgLedger analyses banking messages locally to identify completed purchases and debits, extract amounts and recognise merchants. Spending is shown in Indian rupees, with monthly totals, merchant views and category breakdowns.

Recognised credits, refunds, failed transactions, payment reminders and credit card repayments are excluded from expenses. You can review detected transactions, correct their merchant or category, add missed expenses and remove entries that should not count. Removing an expense recalculates the relevant totals while keeping its original SMS.

Detection depends on the wording used by each sender, so some transactions may need review.

Monthly budgets

Set monthly spending limits for individual categories and receive alerts when a limit is reached or exceeded. Category limits repeat each calendar month, while spending totals start fresh for the new month.

Optional fingerprint protection can be enabled separately for Spending and Budgets.

Credit card organisation

Group recognised credit card messages by issuer and card’s last four digits. Separate views help you find purchases, statements, payment dues, payments and refunds. Where available in an SMS, MsgLedger displays statement amounts, minimum amounts due and payment dates.

Dual SIM support

View messages from both SIMs together or filter the inbox by SIM. Conversations display which SIM received a message, and the composer lets you choose the sending SIM when Android makes that information available.

Personalisation and cleanup

Choose light mode, dark mode or your system setting, along with multiple colour schemes. Compact layouts adapt the interface to smaller displays, including foldable cover screens.

Optional cleanup settings let you delete:
- OTP messages after 1, 2 or 3 days.
- Promotional messages after 1 week or 1 month.
- Old messages after 1 week, 1 month or 2 months.
You can also block senders, review blocked messages, mark all messages as read or use the confirmed delete-all option for a fresh start.

Local processing and optional connectivity

SMS sorting and spending analysis happen on your phone. Contact names and photos are read from Android Contacts with permission and stay on the device. Company logos can be fetched online when available.

The About section includes a live count of participating installations active during the last 30 days. Basic usage reporting is enabled by default and can be switched off; it sends a random installation identifier and app version, without uploading messages, contacts, OTPs or spending records.

MsgLedger works as your default Android SMS app for direct message reception, sending and supported message-management actions. It focuses on SMS, with incoming MMS support; it does not provide RCS messaging.
